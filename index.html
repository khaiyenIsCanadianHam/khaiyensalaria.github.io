01 / AMIYEN

Business mathematics

Amiyen

A browser-based calculator that brings common business mathematics into one workspace. It provides 24 calculation modules, saves inputs and results, and lets users revisit their work through dashboards and CSV exports.

Problem
Business calculations span many formulas, and past results can be difficult to organize.
Solution
A Flask application with dedicated calculator forms and a SQLite-backed calculation history.
  • Python
  • Flask
  • SQLite
  • JavaScript

24 calculator modules · Saved calculation history · Canvas charts · CSV exports

02 / QR ATTENDANCE SYSTEM

Education / attendance

QR Attendance System

A teacher-operated web application for recording student attendance with QR codes. Teachers can check students in, record time-outs, monitor active attendance, and export reports from a protected dashboard.

Problem
Manual attendance records make check-ins, time-outs, and reporting harder to manage.
Solution
A roster-backed QR workflow with teacher authentication, attendance records, and CSV reporting.
  • Python
  • Flask
  • SQLite
  • PostgreSQL (optional)

QR credential generation and revocation · Teacher login · Check-in / time-out · Roster imports

View QR Attendance System repository
